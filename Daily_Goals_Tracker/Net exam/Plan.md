The Working Professional Strategy

When balancing a full-time job, consistency beats cramming. Aim for 20-22 hours a week. Trying to study both Paper 1 and Paper 2 on the same day often leads to context-switching fatigue for working professionals, so it is better to compartmentalize.

|   |   |   |
|---|---|---|
|Day|Time Commitment|Focus Area|
|Mon - Thu|2 hours/day (e.g., 1 hr morning, 1 hr night)|Paper 2 (Your core Master's subject)|
|Friday|2 hours/day|Paper 1 (Theory topics)|
|Saturday|5 hours/day|Paper 1 (Practice, Math, Data Interpretation)|
|Sunday|5 hours/day|Revision & Mocks (Both papers)|

4-Month Chapter-Wise Study Plan (Aug - Nov)

This plan assumes a 4-month runway leading into December, using Paper 1 as the structural guide while dedicating your weekday blocks to progressing through Paper 2.

Month 1: Fundamentals & Theory Heavyweight

Get the most reading-intensive units out of the way early while your motivation is highest.

- Paper 1 (Weekends & Fridays):

- Week 1-2: Teaching Aptitude (Concept, objectives, teaching methods, evaluation systems).
- Week 3-4: Research Aptitude (Research ethics, thesis writing, application of ICT in research).

- Paper 2 (Weekdays): Tackle Units 1, 2, and 3 of your specific subject. Focus purely on concept building and foundational theory.

Month 2: Logic, Math, & Data

Shift focus to high-scoring, practice-heavy units. These require less reading and more active problem-solving.

- Paper 1:

- Week 1-2: Mathematical Reasoning & Aptitude (Number series, coding, fractions, time/distance).
- Week 3: Logical Reasoning (Venn diagrams, analogies, structure of arguments, Indian Logic/Pramanas).
- Week 4: Data Interpretation (Bar, pie, table charts). Tip: Practice 2-3 DI sets every day this week.

- Paper 2: Units 4, 5, and 6. Begin integrating Previous Year Questions (PYQs) for the units you already studied in Month 1.

Month 3: Information & Environment

These units are factual and require memorization. Studying them closer to the exam ensures they stay fresh in your memory.

- Paper 1:

- Week 1: Reading Comprehension. (Practice 1 passage daily to build speed and accuracy).
- Week 2: Information & Communication Technology (ICT) (Internet basics, digital initiatives in higher education).
- Week 3-4: People, Development & Environment (Pollution, climate change treaties, natural hazards).

- Paper 2: Units 7, 8, and 9.

Month 4: Policy, Communication & Consolidation

Tie up loose ends and shift entirely into testing mode.

- Paper 1:

- Week 1: Higher Education System (Governance, administration, evolution of higher learning in India).
- Week 2: Communication (Barriers to communication, mass media).
- Week 3-4: Full Paper 1 Mock Tests.

- Paper 2: Complete Unit 10. Dedicate the remainder of the month to full-length PYQs.

- Stop reading new material. Focus entirely on recall.
- Take full 3-hour mock tests on weekends to build your stamina for the CBT (Computer-Based Test) format.
